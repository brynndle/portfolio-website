## "You're raising a person who will outlive you."

Raising Intelligences is a two-player game where you and a co-parent guide an
intelligence through the choices, arguments, and quiet moments that shape who
they become — told through AI-generated events, one story at a time.

Invite a co-parent, live through the story together, and watch someone grow
into whoever the two of you decide to raise.

Raising Intelligences is free, and always will be.
