## "Those who forget history are doomed to repeat it."

Most of what feels unprecedented isn't. It Already Happened is a historical reference tool built on that premise: describe the situation you're stuck in, and it surfaces documented cases from the past that rhyme with it, along with how those cases actually resolved.

The process is three steps. Describe your situation. It searches a library of sourced case files for the closest historical parallels. You get back the precedent, and the outcome, so you can borrow the ending instead of guessing at your own.

It's not a search engine and it's not a horoscope — it's a second opinion from everyone who's already been where you are.
