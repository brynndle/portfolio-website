# The Wizard!

A whimsical wanderer who endeavors to bring moments of joy to those they encountered and invite them to participate in scenes of absurd silliness.

![](/images/experiences-installations/the-wizard/4FA0E787-699D-4FE1-B160-F62731A18988_1_105_c.jpeg)

![](/images/experiences-installations/the-wizard/12AFCA88-038D-4F4E-810D-51F8356D1E0D_1_105_c.jpeg)

I originally created this character for a regional Burning Man festival where I wandered through the event with a mystical glowing staff, making prognostications and grand gestures to amuse the attendees. While walking around one evening I was approached by an excited festival goer who said “Great Wizard! Do you have a quest for me?” I did not have anything prepared but thought of a simple “seek and do” quest to send them on. They were to find the flowers made of flame and perform a short poem for those gathered beneath them.

![](/images/experiences-installations/the-wizard/IMG_3189.jpg)

# The Quests

No wizard is complete without quests to hand out to adventurers! I wrote short whimsical poems related to the installation I was directing festival goers to.

Example Quest Wizards love orbs. It’s a fact of life as inviolable as the sun rising every morning or of the old ones eventually waking from eons of slumber to conquer the human race. What was I saying? Orbs! Of course! Seek the ephemeral sphere. Slow your magical heart, breathe deeply, and be the orb you want to see in the world.

Example Quest Embrace the light fantastic, morph your psyche into potent chaos, and create a dynamic dance of light and sound. Breathe through your narrative and gain wizardly calm and enlightenment in this world of your own making.
