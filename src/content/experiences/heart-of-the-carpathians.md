# From the Heart of the Forest

### An experience designed to promote the World Wildlife Fund’s Forests Forward Initiative

Created by Cazimi Houdini and Brynn Caputo. [View the slide deck](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1zuhlmFyPza_0bqOYoOGhNt4Ik1GDI3xW/view?usp=sharing)

“Forests Forward is a signature WWF program for corporate action in support of nature, climate, and people. WWF’s local and global experts help companies and organizations unlock the power of forests to achieve ambitious goals with meaningful results.” [WWF: Forests Forward](https://forestsforward.panda.org/)

In this experience for one, the participant will be taken on a journey into the mountains to experience the majesty and culture of the carpathian forests. In the end we hope they will leave with a personal connection to the region and a deeper understanding and appreciation for the ecosystem that we are all a part of. Our experiential aim with this project was to:

Reignite childlike curiosity & wonder through sensory engagement and playful exploration.

Foster & deepen reverence for interconnection by appreciating the web of relationships between the land, its people, and visiting participants.

Shift perspective from stewardship to kinship — reframe our role with nature. “We can protect Nature, but more importantly, Nature protects Us.”
