# Hi! My name is Brynn.

I am a multidisciplinary artist and designer living in the pacific northwest. I am happiest when I’m making tools that help people work better, collaborating with enthusiastic artists and engineers, and creating meaningful things.

In my free time I pour my energy into designing immersive experiences for festivals and local events. I seek to create moments that facilitate transformation for the audience and participants. Constantly curious, I enjoy exploring fringe communities, strange hobbies, and teaching others along the way. Everything I experience gets funneled back into my work. I love creating across mediums and often find that the most powerful inspiration comes from the strangest places.
